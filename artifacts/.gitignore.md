## 1. Overview
Specifies intentionally untracked files and directories that Git should ignore when pushing to GitHub:
- **Virtual Environments**: `venv/`, `.venv/`, `env/`, `ENV/`
- **Python Caches**: `__pycache__/`, `*.py[cod]`, `*$py.class`, `.pytest_cache/`, `.mypy_cache/`, `.ruff_cache/`
- **Build & Distribution**: `dist/`, `build/`, `*.egg-info/`, `*.egg`
- **IDE & OS Metadata**: `.vscode/`, `.idea/`, `*.swp`, `.DS_Store`, `Thumbs.db`, `Desktop.ini`
- **Project Scratch & Temporary Files**: `scratch/`, `test.json`, `*.log`, `*.tmp`, `debug_*.png`, `test_*.py`
- **Secrets & Environment Variables**: `.env`, `.env.*` (excluding `.env.example`)
